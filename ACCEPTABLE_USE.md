# NoClippy - Acceptable Use Policy

NoClippy is a tool for protecting your **personal privacy** while streaming. It lets you hide specific windows from your stream capture so that private information - like DMs, passwords, banking tabs, or unreleased projects - doesn't accidentally appear on screen.

This policy explains what NoClippy is for, what it is not for, and what we expect from everyone who uses it.

---

## What it's for

NoClippy is intended to help streamers and content creators avoid accidental exposure of private, personal content, including:

- Direct messages, emails, or personal chats
- Passwords, authentication tokens, or credential managers
- Banking, financial accounts, or personal documents
- Unreleased or confidential work projects
- Personal photos or files
- Private browser tabs or browsing activity you simply don't want on stream

If you've ever had to scramble to alt-tab away from something personal mid-stream, NoClippy is the tool this policy is about.

---

## What it's NOT for

NoClippy must not be used to conceal, enable, or assist with anything illegal or harmful. The following uses are explicitly prohibited:

- **Hiding illegal content of any kind** - including CSAM, contraband, pirated material, or any content whose mere possession is unlawful
- **Concealing evidence of criminal activity** - fraud, theft, violence, harassment, or any act that violates applicable law
- **Hiding hacking tools, malware, or unauthorized surveillance software**
- **Facilitating cheating, boosting, or deception** on games, platforms, or services in ways that violate their Terms of Service
- **Evading platform moderation** - hiding content that would violate Twitch, YouTube, or any other platform's community guidelines or ToS
- **Deceiving viewers in ways that constitute fraud** or violate consumer protection laws
- **Any use that violates local, national, or international law**

These uses are prohibited regardless of your intent, jurisdiction, or reasoning.

## Reporting misuse

If you believe NoClippy is being used to facilitate illegal activity or harm, please open a GitHub issue at:

**https://github.com/Phaenex/noclippy-releases/issues**

Or email **nick@phaenex.com**.

Include as much context as you can (without including personally identifiable information unnecessarily). Credible reports of illegal use may be escalated to relevant authorities.

NoClippy helps streamers protect **legitimate personal privacy**. It does not exist to help anyone hide illegal content or activity. Use it responsibly.
